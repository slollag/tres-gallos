Welcome to Tres Gallos

Your perfect Cape Cod getaway awaits

Amenities

☕ Coffee

  • A variety of K-cups and sweeteners are available for guest use
  • A drip coffee machine is also available

🛏️ Bedding

  • There is a rollaway bed and portable crib in the upstairs closet, along with additional sheets, blankets and pillows
  • At the end of your stay, please strip the used beds and leave sheets and used towels in a pile on the bedroom floors

🛁 Towels

  • Bath towels are provided in the bathrooms
  • Beach towels are provided in the living area closet
  • At the end of your stay, please leave a pile of the used towels along with used bed sheets on the bedroom floors

🧽 Dishwasher

  • At the end of your stay, please load and run the dishwasher for all used dining and kitchen items
  • Please ensure all used kitchenware, dishware, pots/pans are clean prior to your departure

🧊 Refrigerators

  • There is a full refrigerator in the kitchen, a beverage refrigerator in the living area, and a full freezer in the basement for guest use
  • At the end of your stay, please make sure all refrigerators/freezers are completely empty

🍖 BBQ

  • If the installed propane tank runs out, there is a spare tank in the basement
  • If you used the gas grill during your stay, please scrape the grill clean, and restore the cover

🌡️ HVAC

  • Both second-floor bedrooms have heat and central air conditioning. The thermostat for the second floor is in the Ship Room (north bedroom)
  • The main floor thermostats are in the living areas and the first-floor bedroom suite. The main floor has heat, but not air conditioning. A portable air conditioner is in the main floor bedroom and is controlled locally
  • There are fans in the closets

🔥 Firepit

  • The firepit may only be used on the side yard (down the outdoor steps)
  • Do not leave open flames unattended
  • Extra chairs and supplies are available inside the basement double door
  • Collect and bag cool ashes with household trash; restore the cover atop the fire pit

🎮 Games / Beach Gear / Bikes

  • A variety of games are available in the living areas
  • A variety of beach equipment and bicycles are available in the basement
  • Please return all games & outdoor equipment for the next guest to enjoy

📺 Television & WIFI

  • The living room has a 65-inch television and audio system
  • You may use your streaming services, but please be sure to log out when you done with your rental
  • We do not have a cable television connection
  • Complimentary WIFI available

🗑️ Trash

  • There are bins for recycling and trash on the edge of the driveway
  • Trash pickups are on Wednesdays and recycling pickups are on Thursdays
  • Please leave all trash and recycling in the respective bins at the end of your stay

Questions During Your Stay?

If you have any questions while staying at Tres Gallos, please don't hesitate to reach out.
